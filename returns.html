Return Policy

Our rules for returns, exchanges and refunds.

Return Period

You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving your order if:

  • The product is defective or not working
  • You received the wrong product
  • The product was damaged during delivery

Conditions

To be accepted, a returned product must be:

  • Unused and in its original condition
  • In its original box with all accessories and the invoice

Refunds

Approved refunds are sent through the same payment method used for the order. Delivery charges are not refunded unless the mistake was ours.

Warranty Claims

For faults after the return period, the brand's warranty applies. Keep your invoice and warranty card safe.

How to Request a Return

Contact us with your order details and a photo of the problem. We will reply with the next steps.
